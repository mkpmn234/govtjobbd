# Bangladesh Government Job Circular & Career Resource Directory

A curated repository of official links, exam syllabi, and application guidelines for government job aspirants in Bangladesh.

## 📌 Primary Portal
* **Official Portal:** [Govt Job BD](https://govtjobbd.net) — Daily verified updates on ministries, directorates, and autonomous bodies.
* **All Running Circulars:** [Latest BD Govt Jobs](https://govtjobbd.net)

## 📋 Common Recruitment Categories
- **Civil Service:** Bangladesh Public Service Commission (BPSC / BCS) Circulars & Syllabi
- **Banking Sector:** Bankers' Selection Committee Secretariat (BSCS) & Combined Bank Recruitments
- **Primary & Secondary Education:** Directorate of Primary Education (DPE) Assistant Teacher Recruitment
- **Autonomous & Defense:** Bangladesh Railway, Police, Ansar-VDP, and Tax Zones

## 🛠 Useful Tools for Candidates
- **Teletalk AllJobs Portal:** Official application submission system.
- **Application Deadline Tracker:** Track submission cutoff dates directly on [Govt Job BD](https://govtjobbd.net).
